STANDBY

[MISSION BRIEFING]: A scientist is trapped inside a damaged airlock. The hall is leaking air into the airlock - meaning that the airlock is gaining pressure at the same rate the hall is losing pressure. But, the airlock is also losing pressure to a rupture at the same time.

[OBJECTIVE]: Once you begin, you will receive the initial pressures of each room, and the leak rates. Calculate the exact time (in seconds) from the initial breach when the hall and airlock pressures will equalize so you can safely cycle the doors.

[WARNING]: You have a strict time limit before pressures diverge past safe thresholds, and only 3 manual override attempts.

Calculate the exact time (in seconds) when the hall and airlock pressures equalize so the doors can be safely cycled.
