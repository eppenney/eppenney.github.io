TRACTOR BEAM :: ESCAPE POD INTERCEPT

STANDBY

[MISSION BRIEFING]: An escape pod was ejected from the station into open space and is currently drifting away at a constant velocity.

[OBJECTIVE]: The ship's tractor beam requires a specific charge duration before it can teleport the pod back. Once diagnostic initializes, calculate how far away (in meters) the escape pod will be from the station when the beam finishes charging and recharges.

[WARNING]: You have a strict time limit before the pod drifts out of maximum sensor range, and only 3 manual calculation attempts.

[CRITICAL ALERT]: Escape pod was ejected 12 seconds ago, and has been traveling at 3 meters / second. The tractor beam will take 90 seconds to charge.

Determine how many meters away from the ship the escape pod will be when the tractor beam recharges (at total time C = A + B seconds from ejection).
