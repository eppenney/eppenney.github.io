Post #142 — Strange Men

User: ConduitionalPoet | Date: 1987-02-19 | Tags: Mystery, Kingswood, Local

This is going to sound crazy, but has anyone else been around Kingswood Camp this winter? I’ve been doing some photography out that way — mostly trying to get shots of the frozen lake and the cabins under snow.

A few times now, I’ve seen figures out there after dark. Not kids, not staff, not locals I recognize. Always men. Always in dark suits. At first I thought they were security, but there’s no reason for that place to have guards, especially off-season.

Last night I was near the old dining hall and saw two of them standing on the hill where the campfire ring used to be. No flashlights. Just standing there, watching toward the lake. When I tried to approach, they turned away in unison and walked back toward the director’s house. By the time I got there, they were gone. No tracks.

Has anyone else been seeing this? I know some of you have ties to the camp or the area. I’m not trying to stir things up, but something’s off out there this winter.
