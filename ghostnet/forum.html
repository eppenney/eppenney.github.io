Abnormal Radio Interference on Lake George

User: KnotaFed | Date: 2017-11-28| Tags: ghost, mystery

Hello everyone. I’m back from an extended trip with my partner and have some things of interest to share. We travelled to a delightful campsite in the province of Nova Scotia, on Lake George with some friends.
Winter festivities aside, I had some downtime listening to the radio (if you haven’t read my blog – fuck Spotify and all the ‘streaming’ corporations like them.) I know that I said before I left that I would be taking a break from all this, but what I found was too interesting to pass up. While listening to some music, I started to get interference from some sort of local FM broadcast. Not unusual to be honest, plenty of churches broadcast over frequencies with higher power setups than they’re supposed to.
The thing is – I can’t explain what it is that I heard. It sounded like a woman crying – just quietly sobbing. I had to turn the volume up as loud as it could go to hear it. After about three minutes, the signal went dead. I noted the time, 9:03 PM. I monitored that same frequency, and at 9:30 PM exactly I caught it again. For three minutes, I could just barely hear a woman sobbing. With my headphones I was able to get some more detail. At 9:32:36 there was a sound like metal being hit underwater, and the voice said something. I can’t be sure, but it sounded like the name “Madeline” with the intonation of a question.
At this point, I was invested. I got my recording equipment from the car, and was an anti-social shut in for the rest of the night. At 10:00PM the signal came back. I was able to capture this recording.
I was chilled to say the least. I promised my partner that I would take a break from my research for this trip, so that was all I was able to capture. Let me know what you think! Has any else been up to Lake George?

The Hairy Man (Not a Joke)

User: GhostFVcker | Date: 1991-01-08 | Tags: Creature, Sighting

Okay so this isn’t a bit. I was driving down Aylesford last night, snow coming down hard, and this thing ran across in front of my car. Looked like a man, but… not.

Tall. Built like a wrestler – like Hulk Hogan built. Hair all over. Every inch. Barefoot. No coat, no pants, just skin and muscle and I could almost see steam rising off him. Could see muscles move under my headlights, rippling – I’ve never seen anything so strong. The things a creature like that could do to me... And I swear, he looked back at me as he crossed — like he knew me. I felt it – a connection. My heart was beating. I know it was just a moment, but it felt… longer. His whole… you know… was out and on display. The image is burned into my brain – the size of my forearm. I swear.

Whole thing lasted two seconds but I can’t stop thinking about it. About him. His hairy, muscular bod. My hands were shaking. My heart’s still pounding remembering the way it moved — fast, confident, powerful – a real alpha of a creature. I pulled over, sat there ten minutes trying to catch my breath, windows fogged up, trying to see where he went. I had a dream about him last night, about his hands around my neck… Fuck.

Anyway. Has anyone else seen him?
